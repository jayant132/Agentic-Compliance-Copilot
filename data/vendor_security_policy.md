# Vendor Security Policy

This policy defines the security requirements for third-party vendors with access to company systems or data.

## Vendor Risk Assessment
All vendors with access to Confidential or Restricted data must complete a security risk assessment before onboarding.

## Contractual Requirements
Vendor contracts must include data protection clauses, breach notification obligations, and the right to audit.

## Ongoing Monitoring
Critical vendors must be reassessed for security risk at least annually.

## Vendor Offboarding
Vendor access to company systems must be revoked within 5 business days of contract termination.
