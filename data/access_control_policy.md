# Access Control Policy

This policy governs how access to company systems and data is granted, reviewed, and revoked.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all access to production systems, cloud infrastructure, and any system storing Confidential or Restricted data.

## Least Privilege
Access must be granted based on the principle of least privilege. Users must only receive the minimum access required to perform their job function.

## Access Reviews
Access rights for all production systems must be reviewed quarterly by the system owner. Any unnecessary access must be revoked within 5 business days of identification.

## Offboarding
All system access for departing employees must be revoked within 24 hours of their termination date.
