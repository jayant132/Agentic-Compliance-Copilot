# Information Security Policy

This policy defines the baseline information security requirements for all employees, contractors, and systems.

## Data Classification
All company data must be classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.

## Acceptable Use
Company systems must only be used for authorized business purposes. Installation of unapproved software on company devices is prohibited without IT Security approval.

## Security Awareness Training
All employees must complete security awareness training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter. Training completion must be logged and auditable.

## Policy Review
This policy must be reviewed and approved by the CISO at least once every 12 months.
