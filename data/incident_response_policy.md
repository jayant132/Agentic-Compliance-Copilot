# Incident Response Policy

This policy defines how the company detects, responds to, and reports security incidents.

## Incident Reporting
All suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security team within 1 hour of detection.

## Incident Response Plan
The company must maintain a documented incident response plan that is tested at least once per year through a tabletop exercise.

## Breach Notification
If a confirmed data breach involves personal data, affected individuals and relevant regulators must be notified within 72 hours of confirmation.

## Post-Incident Review
A post-incident review must be conducted within 10 business days of incident closure to identify root cause and remediation actions.
