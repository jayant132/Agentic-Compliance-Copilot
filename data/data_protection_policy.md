# Data Protection Policy

This policy defines how personal and sensitive company data must be handled.

## Data Minimization
Only the minimum personal data necessary for a defined business purpose may be collected and retained.

## Encryption
All Confidential and Restricted data, including personal data, must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## Data Retention
Personal data must not be retained longer than necessary for its original purpose, and retention periods must be documented per data category.

## Data Subject Requests
Requests from individuals to access, correct, or delete their personal data must be fulfilled within 30 days.
